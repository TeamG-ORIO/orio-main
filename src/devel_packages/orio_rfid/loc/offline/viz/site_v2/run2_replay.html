What the dots and ellipsoids are

The dot is the method's own position estimate, the one the reported errors are measured from. F1 takes the most likely cell and averages the belief within 30 cm of it. F2 to F4 take the place with the most probability nearby (the belief blurred by 20 cm) and average within 20 cm.

Each ellipsoid stands for one connected blob of the tag's 50% or 90% most likely region. Up to 3 blobs per tag; a blob is drawn only if it holds at least about 3% (50% region) or 5% (90% region) of the probability. Centre and orientation come from the blob's weighted mean and covariance; the volume equals the blob's volume, then each axis is capped at the blob's 95th-percentile extent.

They are not Gaussian σ-ellipses. Before the regions are cut, each belief is widened by a factor chosen once per method on the dev items so that 90% regions hold the truth about 90% of the time at the end of the run. Mid-run coverage was not checked.

Evidence, all four methods: the learned read/RSSI model (reads and non-reads), phase within each channel dwell kept per height because the antenna moves, and a learned end-to-end network. Each tag's models never saw that part of the room. Tags attached to the same object share their evidence in F1 to F3.
Causal replay: every term is rebuilt from the data recorded up to the shown moment, in 2 s steps. The end-to-end network was trained on whole passes, so its output in the middle of a pass is rougher than at the end. A tag gets a belief once it has 5 reads.
